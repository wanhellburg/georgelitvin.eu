George Litvin (Egor)

CULTURAL ORGANIZER / RESEARCHER

George Litvin (Egor) is a cultural organizer and researcher working at the intersection of contemporary culture, research, and civic initiatives.

Born on 16 April 1998 in Moscow. Since 2016, he has been based in Prague, where he develops and participates in cultural, educational, and public projects.

He is a co-organizer of KULTURUS, a Prague-based festival of contemporary culture and human rights that brings together artists, researchers, activists, and independent cultural initiatives.

George conducts independent research and fieldwork, exploring how people, places, and traditions shape contemporary societies. He has carried out expeditions in India, Japan, and Morocco, visited more than 75 countries, and works on projects related to culture, identity, and social change.
